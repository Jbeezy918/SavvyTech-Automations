Title: Customer Refund Process
Version: 2.1
Effective Date: 2026-01-15
Owner: Finance Team
Department: Accounts Receivable
Purpose: Standardize how customer refunds are reviewed, approved, and issued.

Preconditions:
- Customer has a valid, paid order
- Refund request has supervisor approval on file

Required Systems:
- Billing Portal
- Email

Required Forms:
- Refund Authorization Form

1. Open the Billing Portal and locate the customer order.
2. Verify the refund amount matches the approved authorization.
   Warning: Never issue more than the approved amount.
3. If the refund is over $500, decide whether a second approval is required.
4. Enter the refund in the Billing Portal.
5. Send the customer a confirmation email.
   Expected Output: Confirmation number recorded on the order.
